03 / Client experience

A private project collaboration room

Client portal workflow

  • Admin creates a client login and assigns projects.
  • Clients see progress, deadlines, latest updates, files, and billing.
  • Daily updates can include a working video, market context, next action, and a decision prompt.
  • Clients can respond securely to a project update from their portal.
  • Portal refreshes manually or automatically during the workday.
  • Logout now returns clients to the public homepage instead of the login page.

Email notifications

New project updates can notify the client through Resend with the update summary, video link, market context, next action, decision prompt, and portal link. Email failures do not prevent updates from saving.
